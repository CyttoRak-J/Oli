Support

We're happy to help. Here's how to reach us.

How to get help

The fastest way is to open an issue on GitHub:

https://github.com/CyttosPlay/CyttosPlay/issues

  • Choose Bug report for something that doesn't work.
  • Choose Feature request for ideas and improvements.

Writing a good bug report

  1. Say what you did. The exact steps that led to the problem (e.g. "Pasted a YouTube link in Search, pressed Enter, nothing happened").
  2. Say what you expected and what actually happened.
  3. Attach the log file. Found in %APPDATA%\Oli\logs — the newest cytto-*.log file. It tells us exactly what the app was doing.
  4. Mention your Oli version (shown in Settings → About) and Windows version.
Before reporting, check the Troubleshooting page — your issue may already have a fix, and a quick check of the log file can save a round trip.

Feature requests

Ideas are welcome. Tell us what you want Oli to do and how you'd use it. Popular requests get built first.

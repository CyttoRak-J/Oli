Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the questions we hear most.

Where does Oli store my data?

In one SQLite database file: %APPDATA%\Oli\library.sqlite. Your library scan data, settings, queue, history, favorites, and playlists all live there. Download artwork is cached in the same folder.

Does Oli upload anything to the internet?

Oli works offline for your local music. Internet is only used when you search YouTube, download videos, or fetch lyrics/artwork for online tracks. Your library, settings, and personal data stay on your computer.

Does Oli need yt-dlp or ffmpeg installed?

yt-dlp is bundled with Oli — nothing to install. ffmpeg is only needed for video downloads and is picked up from your system if present (winget install ffmpeg if not).

Can I download YouTube songs as audio files?

Yes. Paste a video link in the Search page and choose Song — Oli downloads the best audio and tags it with title, artist, cover art, and more. Whole playlists work too.

Which audio formats are supported?

MP3, FLAC, AAC/M4A, WAV, OGG/Opus, and more — anything your Windows media pipeline can decode. Metadata is read automatically, including embedded artwork.

Can I use Oli with Spotify?

Oli supports Spotify as a search provider for playing music online. If you have a Spotify Client ID and Client Secret, you can enter them in Settings → Providers for higher-rate search results. They are stored only on your computer.

How do I keep Oli running in the background?

Enable Settings → Close to tray. Closing the window then keeps Oli running with playback and taskbar controls; the tray icon (bottom-right) is where you'll find it.

Will my settings survive updates?

Yes. Updates install over the app and never touch your data folder. Oli also includes automatic recovery and one-time restore logic for older data folders, so your theme, queue, and history are preserved.

Welcome to Oli

Oli is a premium desktop music player and local library manager for Windows. Here you'll find everything you need to get started.

What Oli can do

Local library YouTube playback & downloads Smart playlists Mini player Lyrics Queue & history

  • Play your own music collection with full metadata, artwork, and gapless control.
  • Search and play YouTube videos, or download them as video or tagged audio files.
  • Queue, history, favorites, and smart playlists — everything is saved locally.
  • Dark theme (including OLED), mini player, taskbar controls, and tray integration.

Getting started

  1. Install Oli. Download Oli-Setup-1.0.0.exe from the Releases page and run it. No additional runtime is required.
  2. Add your music. Open Settings → Library and add the folder with your music (for example A:\Flac). Oli scans it automatically and keeps it up to date.
  3. Start listening. Browse by songs, albums, or artists — or paste a YouTube link into the Search page to play or download it.
  4. Make it yours. Pick an accent color and theme, adjust playback, enable the mini player, and organize your queue.
